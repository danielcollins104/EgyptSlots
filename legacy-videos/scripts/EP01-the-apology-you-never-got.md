# EP01 — "The Apology You Never Got"

**Length target:** ~75 seconds | **Anchor:** Romans 12:19

## Voiceover script

Somebody hurt you, and they never said sorry.

Maybe it was years ago. A parent. A friend. A spouse.
And part of you is still standing at the door, waiting for an apology
that may never come.

Here's the hard, freeing truth:
you don't need their apology to be free.

Romans 12:19 says — "Do not take revenge, my dear friends,
but leave room for God's wrath, for it is written:
'It is mine to avenge; I will repay,' says the Lord."

Hear what that means. The debt is real. God sees it.
But it's His to settle — not yours to carry.

Forgiving someone who never apologized isn't saying it didn't matter.
It's handing the weight to the only One strong enough to hold it.

So here's your one thing today:
Say their name — out loud, or in your heart — and say:
"I release you. You don't owe me anymore."

It might take a hundred repetitions. That's okay.
Freedom usually does.

Father, for everyone carrying an apology that never came —
give them the courage to set it down today. In Jesus' name, Amen.
